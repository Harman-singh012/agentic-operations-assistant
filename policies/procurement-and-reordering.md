# Procurement and Reordering Policy (sample)

A product needs reordering when its quantity on hand is at or below its reorder level. Operations raises a purchase order (PO) within 2 business days of a product hitting that threshold.

Standard reorder quantity is enough stock to cover 8 weeks of average demand. Server and networking products may be ordered at 4 weeks of demand because of high unit value.

PO approval limits: up to USD 5,000 is approved by the Operations Manager; USD 5,001 to USD 25,000 requires the Finance Director; above USD 25,000 requires CFO approval. Orders must not be split to avoid these limits.

Orders must use an approved supplier. Quotes in foreign currency are converted to USD at the rate on the day the PO is raised for approval purposes.

Warehouse transfers between sites do not need a PO but must be recorded in the inventory system the same day.
