# Returns and RMA Policy (sample)

Customers may return unopened hardware within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened, non-defective hardware returned within 30 days is subject to a 15% restocking fee.

Hardware that is dead on arrival or defective must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Every return needs an RMA number issued by Customer Operations before shipping; returns without an RMA are refused at the warehouse.

Laptops and workstations that fail within the 12-month standard warranty are repaired or replaced by the supplier. Operations must log the serial number and fault description on the RMA form.

Returned stock is inspected within 3 business days. Items that pass inspection are returned to sellable inventory in the originating warehouse.
